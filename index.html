HRCI (Human Rights Council of India) membership offers conscious citizens, advocates, doctors, educators, and social contributors an official national platform. As an active member, you participate directly in defending constitutional rights, organizing legal awareness camps, and delivering grassroots community welfare across 28 states of India.

Annual Membership is valid for 1 full year from the date of official issuance. Lifetime Membership grants permanent, lifelong affiliation with supreme organizational privileges and zero renewal requirement. Executive & Leadership Memberships are assigned based on institutional role and committee tenure.

Any Indian citizen aged 18 years or above with a clean track record, who is committed to constitutional values, justice, equality, and social service, is eligible to apply for HRCI membership through our official verification process.

Annual Membership requires a yearly renewal to maintain active digital credentials. Lifetime Members enjoy permanent status with zero recurring renewal fees and lifetime access to national summits, conventions, and welfare programs.

Upon successful verification and approval of your enrollment, an instant QR-verified Digital Membership ID Card and Certificate are issued immediately to your WhatsApp and registered email. A physical embossed PVC Smart Card is subsequently dispatched to your postal address.
