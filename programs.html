Our Programs & Initiatives

Grassroots social work, legal counseling, and humanitarian campaigns protecting the defenseless.

Pillar 1 • Judicial Access

Free Legal Aid & Awareness Camps

HRCI's Legal Taskforce unites senior advocates and retired magistrates to provide free legal advice, draft public interest litigations (PILs), and assist underprivileged citizens who cannot afford high court or supreme court representation.

Request Legal Help →
Pillar 2 • Youth Empowerment

Training & Human Rights Advocacy Fellowships

We train youth, law students, and social workers through specialized diploma workshops covering constitutional rights, FIR filing procedures, police complaint redressal, and human rights advocacy frameworks.

Join as Youth Advocate →
Pillar 3 • Protection

Women Safety & Anti-Harassment Cells

Dedicated state units for women protection that counsel victims of domestic violence, workplace harassment, and dowry abuse. We work in close coordination with District Police Authorities and State Women Commissions.

Helpline: +91-98765-43210
Pillar 4 • Humanitarian Care

Social Welfare, Health & Disaster Relief

Organizing blood donation camps, free healthcare check-ups for rural citizens, emergency relief during floods and natural disasters, and distributing warm clothes and educational kits to orphaned children.
