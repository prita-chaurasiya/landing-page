About Human Rights Council of India

A National, Non-Political & Secular Organization safeguarding constitutional dignity, legal rights, and social justice across 28 States of India.

The Organization's Genesis & Authority

The Human Rights Council of India (HRCI) / भारतीय मानवाधिकार परिषद was formally established on 11 January 2013 as a national entity to champion the civil liberties enshrined in the Constitution of India and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR).

Legally incorporated under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India as a Section 8 Non-Profit Company, HRCI maintains a strictly non-political, secular, and democratic character with active working committees across 28 states and union territories.

Mission, Vision & Core Principles

🎯

Our Mission

To actively identify, prevent, and legally resolve human rights violations, and to provide accessible legal counseling and humanitarian aid to marginalized citizens.

👁️

Our Vision

An inclusive India where every citizen exercises their fundamental rights with dignity, free from prejudice, bureaucratic apathy, or socioeconomic injustice.

⚖️

Our Core Values

Absolute impartiality, secular integrity, respect for the rule of law, zero tolerance for discrimination, and grassroots community service.
